Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a psychic reading cost?
New customers pay $1.00 per minute. You are billed by the minute and can hang up any time, so you only pay for the time you use. Standard rates apply after your introductory call.
How fast will you call me?
Usually within five minutes of leaving your name and number in the "we'll call you" form. If you'd rather not wait, call (555) 010-0101 directly.
Who will I be speaking with?
An AI voice advisor. See the note in "What is Manhattan Psychics?" above. Your reading is private and never shared.
What if I'm not happy with my reading?
Contact us within 24 hours and we'll credit the call. That's our satisfaction guarantee.
What should I ask during my reading?
Anything on your mind: a relationship, an ex, a decision at work, a move, a feeling you can't shake. Specific questions get specific answers.
Can I get a reading anytime?
Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.
Is it private?
Yes. Phone only, no camera, no app. Your number is used to connect the call and for nothing else.
