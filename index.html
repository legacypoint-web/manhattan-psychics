What can I expect from a psychic reading?
A private phone call, as long or short as you like. Ask about a relationship, a decision, a feeling you can't shake. You'll get direct answers and hang up when you're done.
How can I prepare for my psychic reading?
Find a quiet spot and have one or two specific questions ready. Specific questions get specific answers.
What should I ask during my psychic reading?
Anything on your mind: an ex, a new relationship, a job, a move, family, money.
What if I'm not happy with my psychic reading?
Contact us within 24 hours and we'll credit the call. That's our Life-Changing or FREE Guarantee.
