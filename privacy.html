  • To connect your call or call you back when you ask us to.

  • To respond to customer care requests.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with the law.
  • To measure and improve the site, the service, and our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the line and the site, such as telephony carriers, speech and AI processing providers, payment processors, hosting, and analytics providers, and only as needed for them to perform those services.
